## Profile

My primary work is Java/Spring backend engineering: business logic, REST APIs, integrations, databases, messaging, migrations, security, testing and operational concerns around long-lived services.

I care especially about the properties that help a system survive beyond its first implementation: explicit ownership boundaries, controlled migrations, observability, CI/CD and the ability to verify changes safely.
